# Rosalind Workbench recipes

Each recipe starts with a focused Codex prompt and names the plugins used for
that part of the work. Combine recipes in the order that fits the materials you
have: a census can lead to cell-population comparison or structure review; a
selected site can lead to a supplied-library screen or a binder campaign.

```text
Disease and surface census
        ├─ Compare cell populations
        └─ Sequences, structures, and sites
                 ├─ Supplied libraries and screening
                 └─ Protein-binder design
                              ↓
                    Experiments and report
```

- [Compare cell populations](cell-populations.md)
- [Sequences, structures, and sites](sequences-structures-sites.md)
- [Supplied libraries and screening](supplied-libraries-screening.md)
- [Protein-binder design](protein-binder-design.md)
- [Experiments and report](experiments-and-report.md)
- [Publish a report as a ChatGPT Site](chatgpt-site.md)

Use [adversarial review](../adversarial-review.md) at the relevant decision
points. Independent reviewers check the evidence, target and construct
identity, controls, and report claims. The guide includes a prompt for
parallel subagent review and explains how to resolve findings.
