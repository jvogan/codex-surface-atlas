# Evidence and decisions

For each selected target and molecule format, record the supporting findings,
conflicting evidence, and measurements still needed. Label each finding by how
it was obtained.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Observed | Directly stated or measured by a cited source. |
| Derived | Deterministic transformation of recorded observations. |
| Inferred | Interpretation that states its assumptions. |
| Proposed | A research or therapeutic hypothesis to be evaluated. |
| Not measured | Relevant information was unavailable or not established in the recorded scope. |
| Not applicable | The field does not apply to this entity or modality. |

Record missing values as missing. Reserve zero and false for results that the
source actually reports.

## Three views of each target

### Target evidence

Compare disease relevance, cell population, direct and indirect surface
evidence, patient or model coverage, and functional measurements. Include
conflicting results and source limitations.

### Normal-tissue and biological risk

Check normal-tissue distribution, essential functions, and which cell types
could be exposed to the intervention. Record shedding, soluble forms, variation
between cells or patients, isoforms, modifications, and accessibility.

### Action and modality fit

Compare molecule formats for the intended action: blockade, agonism, payload
delivery, imaging, ligand trapping, immune engagement, or small-molecule
modulation. Record the required binding site, supporting structure, controls,
and measurements still needed.

## Structures and results

Label each structure as experimental, predicted, or derived, and identify its
binding partners. Present computed poses and designs as hypotheses, with their
model settings, controls, and evaluation results.

## Decision records

Record one target, intended action, and molecule format per opportunity.
Include the reason for selection, risks, binding site, supporting structures
and sources, and the proposed analysis or experiment. Keep these fields
available alongside any score used to rank the opportunity.
