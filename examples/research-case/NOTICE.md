# Research example notice

This example contains selected evidence and computational results for CEACAM5,
ITGB6, and FAP. Its research coverage is partial.

## Scientific sources

- **Cancer Surfaceome Atlas:** selected factual measurements from Table S6 of
  [Hu et al. (2021), *Nature Cancer* 2, 1406–1422](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43018-021-00282-w)
  support the target records. The example selects these measurements and adds
  derived scores and context annotations; it does not reproduce the full table,
  article, or article figures. The source material retains its original terms.
- **UniProt:** selected identity and topology annotations for
  [CEACAM5 / P06731](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P06731/entry),
  [ITGB6 / P18564](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P18564/entry), and
  [FAP / Q12884](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q12884/entry).
  The example summarizes these annotations. Copyrightable portions of the
  UniProt databases are available under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://www.uniprot.org/help/license/).
- **Protein Data Bank:** deposited coordinates from
  [8BW0](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8BW0),
  [8TCG](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8TCG), and
  [6Y0F](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6Y0F). Worldwide PDB archive data are
  distributed under [CC0](https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies).
- **PubChem:** compound identity records cite
  [EGCG / CID 65064](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/65064),
  [cilengitide / CID 176873](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/176873), and
  [linagliptin / CID 10096344](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10096344).
  Source terms depend on the contributing record; see
  [PubChem's download guidance](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/downloads).

## Prepared and generated files

Files labeled `deposited` contain experimental archive coordinates or renders
of them. Files labeled `derived-target-only` contain a target prepared from a
deposited complex. PyMOL figures show the packaged structures; preview
descriptors record the figure selections and source files.

Files labeled `docked`, `predicted`, or `generated` contain computational
hypotheses. The example identifies Smina/Vinardo docking, BoltzGen design, and
Boltz2 complex predictions in the corresponding records. The CEACAM5 design
was not promoted because its control assessment failed.
